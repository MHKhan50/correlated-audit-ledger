# Dataset card

`synthetic_test_dataset.csv` is generated by `run_experiment.py`. It contains simulated episode-level labels and observed evidence scores, across independent field-missingness and shared-agent-outage settings. It is not collected from real users, deployed systems, or LLMs. The data are deterministic for the fixed seeds in the script. Use for teaching and method prototyping only; do not make real-world safety claims from it.
